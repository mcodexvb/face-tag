# Recorded samples

- `piano/`: Splendid Grand Piano (AKAI Steinway), public domain. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano via https://github.com/danigb/samples. Two loudness layers (soft, loud), named by MIDI note, trimmed to 3.2 s, mono AAC.
- `drums/`: Virtuosity Drums by Versilian Studios, played by Austin McMahon, CC0 1.0. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums. Kick, snare, two toms, closed hi-hat, crash and ride from the "mid" microphone, two loudness layers each, trimmed, mono AAC.

The harmonium and tabla are synthesised in the browser (no freely licensed recordings were used).
